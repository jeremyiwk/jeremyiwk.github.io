Bio

Math, physics, and computation

I’m a research engineer based in Los Angeles.

Current Work

At Metalenz, I work on computational photonics and metasurface design. This includes building electromagnetic simulations for GPUs and tools for gradient-based inverse design.

Previous Work

As a computational scientist at Thermo Fisher Scientific, I developed algorithms for focused ion beam alignment, differentiable charged-particle simulations, and models of ion beam milling.

I studied mathematics and physics at the University of Oregon, earning a bachelor’s degree in 2020 and a master’s in physics in 2022. My graduate research used molecular dynamics simulations to develop models of DNA and protein dynamics.

Areas of focus

  • Computational physics
  • Differentiable simulation
  • Numerical optimization
  • GPU computing
